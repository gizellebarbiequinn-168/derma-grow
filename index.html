TREND DEBUNKER

The DIY Lemon Juice Trend

Applying raw lemon juice strips your natural acid mantle (~4.5 pH) due to its extreme acidity (~2.0 pH), inducing chemical irritation and hyperpigmentation.

TREND DEBUNKER

Toothpaste on Acne Blemishes

Sodium lauryl sulfate and hydrogen peroxide in toothpaste burn delicate facial epithelial tissues, triggering severe secondary hyperpigmentation.

TREND DEBUNKER

Coarse Walnut Shell Scrubs

Crushed walnut shells feature microscopic jagged edges that create micro-tears in stratum corneum layers, compromising moisture retention.

PRODUCT CATEGORY

Low-pH Gel Cleansers vs. Foaming Bars

Slightly acidic cleansers (pH 5.0–5.5) maintain microbiome balance, while alkaline bar soaps disrupt protective intercellular lipids.

PRODUCT CATEGORY

Chemical vs. Mineral Sunscreen Agents

Zinc oxide physically reflects broad-spectrum rays, making it gentler for reactive barrier types compared to high-percentage chemical filters.

SKINCARE INGREDIENT

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) Mechanism

Inhibits melanosome transfer and boosts ceramide synthesis, enhancing resilience against daily transepidermal water loss.

SKINCARE INGREDIENT

Glycerin & Multi-Molecular Humectants

Draws ambient environmental moisture into upper epidermal cells, accelerating lipid matrix barrier repairs.

SKIN BIOLOGY

Understanding Stratum Corneum & Acid Mantle

The outermost brick-and-mortar layer of keratinocytes and ceramides prevents microbial invasion and controls moisture loss.

SKIN BIOLOGY

Transepidermal Water Loss (TEWL) Pathways

Occurs when ambient dryness or over-exfoliation weakens intercellular lipid bonds, leading to inflammation and flaking.
