Search common skincare ingredients, product formulations, and skin biology terms in plain English.

Hyaluronic Acid

Active Component

A moisture-binding molecule that holds up to 1000x its weight in water to plump the skin surface.

🧠 Pro Insight: Apply to damp skin to prevent drawing moisture outward.

Niacinamide

Active Component

Vitamin B3 compound that strengthens the barrier, limits excess sebum production, and unifies tone.

🧠 Pro Insight: Mixes smoothly with most actives without causing flares.

Retinol

Active Component

Vitamin A derivative that accelerates cell turnover and stimulates structural collagen paths.

🧠 Pro Insight: Use strictly at night and wear broad-spectrum protection by day.

Salicylic Acid

Active Component

Oil-soluble Beta Hydroxy Acid that penetrates pore walls to dissolve sebum plugs.

🧠 Pro Insight: Best suited for breakout-prone oily skin zones.
